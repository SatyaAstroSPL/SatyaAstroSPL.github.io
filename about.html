About Me

Hello! I'm Satyapriya, a Scientist at the Space Physics Laboratory, VSSC, ISRO. My journey in astrophysics began with a fascination for the night sky, which led me to pursue an M.S. in Astronomy & Astrophysics at IIST.

Currently, I am working as a Scientist on AI/ML in space weather & instrumentation part, focusing on developing advanced data analysis techniques for space missions.
